Nibra turns your speech into text directly on your device. Type less and dictate more, into any input field you are using.
A large recording area shows the input level and stops automatically when you pause. Your text is saved in a local, searchable history and can be inserted into other apps whenever needed.
Through the accessibility service, Nibra pastes text exactly where your cursor is. Each entry can be edited later and recognized again in a different language.
Text snippets replace frequently used words or phrases with your own shortcuts. A privacy screen hides the content from prying eyes when needed.
No data is sent over the network. The app requires no internet permission, no account, shows no ads, and contains no trackers.
