## What is no longer here

61 images from before the rename (commit `e9dc476`) were removed. They did
not show this app:

- the **template** AIDictation — orange waveform logo, English interface,
  someone else's name. That such material sat in the tree surfaced twice:
  once here, and once as a complete store presence under `app/src/main/play/`
- intermediate states under the old name **Loqui**, some already in Nibra's
  design but with "Loqui, version 1.0" in the brand footer

Neither works as evidence for the current state nor as an illustration.
Anyone who needs them will find them in the version history.

The two images under `lauf-2-5-feldschutz/` also predate the rename. They
stay because they prove a **behaviour** — bubble yes, password field no — and
the name does not change that.
